ON DELETE choices:
After a bit of research and revision we followed this rules to apply the on delete choiuces:
CASCADE:The row means nothing without its parent, so it is deleted with it.
NO ACTION:Deleting the parent would lose history or leave a required value empty, so the delete is refused until the rows that use it are changed first.
SET NULL:The approver is optional: if the approver is deleted, the reservation stay and only the approver becomes empty.

OPEN QUESTIONS:
1. Administrative staff. Administrative employees cannot make reservations or participate in projects. A foreign key can check that something exists, but not that a person is NOT in the Administrative table.
2. Certifications and one user at a time. A person can only reserve a unit if they hold all the certifications its model requires, and a unit cannot be used by two people at the same time. Both rules compare several rows of Requires, Holds and Reservation.
3. "Exactly one" and "at least one". A person must be a Student or an Employee but not both, every student needs at least one advisor, and every project and laboratory needs at least one budget. Separate tables cannot stop a person from being in both subclasses, and a foreign key cannot force another table to have a mattching row.
